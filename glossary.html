Addiction
A condition where a person keeps using a substance or behavior even when it causes harm, often involving changes in brain reward pathways.
Dopamine
A brain chemical linked to motivation and reward. It helps explain why some habits feel hard to stop.
Tolerance
Needing more of a substance or activity over time to feel the same effect.
Withdrawal
Physical or emotional symptoms that can appear when someone suddenly reduces or stops a substance or habit.
Stigma
Negative judgment or shame that can stop people from seeking help or learning accurate information.
Digital addiction
A popular term for compulsive screen or app use that interferes with sleep, school, work, or relationships.
